# Contributing to CutView

Open an issue with your Windows version, video container and codec if known, steps to reproduce the problem, and what you expected. Use a synthetic or redistributable clip when an example is needed.

For a pull request, follow the [local setup](README.md#run-from-source). Describe what changed and what you checked. For playback or export changes, check opening a clip, trimming it, and playing the exported copy.
